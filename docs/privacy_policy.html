Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what ParkLagbee collects to connect Vehicle Owners with Spot Owners, how that information is used and shared, and how you can request deletion.

1. Information we collect

Information depends on the features you use. It may include:

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, and the address or area you provide.
  • Identity verification: NID or Driving License number and images of verification documents you choose to upload.
  • Vehicle and booking information: vehicle type and registration number, booking dates and times, selected spot, and booking status.
  • Spot listing information: listing description, address, availability, pricing, contact details, map information, and photos uploaded by Spot Owners.
  • App activity: reviews, support requests, notifications, and information needed to manage bookings and prevent abuse.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify users where applicable, display and manage parking listings, process booking requests, communicate booking updates, provide support, improve reliability, and investigate reports or safety issues.

3. Location information

When location access is available and you choose to allow it, ParkLagbee uses device location only while you are using the app to show nearby available parking and calculate distance. We do not track device location in the background. Spot addresses and map coordinates that a Spot Owner submits are used to describe and locate that listing.

4. How information is shared

We share information when needed to provide the service. Listing details and uploaded spot photos may be visible to app users. Booking details and relevant contact information may be shared between the Vehicle Owner and Spot Owner involved in a booking. We also use Supabase to provide authentication, database, and file storage services. We may disclose information when required by law or to address security, fraud, or safety concerns.

5. Storage and security

Account, booking, and listing data are stored in the services configured for ParkLagbee. Verification documents are stored separately from public listing photos with access controls. We apply access restrictions and reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide ParkLagbee, meet applicable legal or financial obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. You can permanently delete your account in Profile settings. You can also submit a request through the account deletion page. Profile data, listings, verification documents, uploaded spot photos, and account-linked history are deleted when a verified request is completed, except records that must be retained for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Your choices

You can review or update some profile information in the app, choose whether to provide optional information, and use the in-app Help & Report Issue option to contact support. You may deny a permission; features that depend on it may not be available.

8. Children and policy changes

ParkLagbee is intended for adults arranging parking services and is not designed for children. We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use Help & Report Issue in the app or submit a request through the account deletion page.
