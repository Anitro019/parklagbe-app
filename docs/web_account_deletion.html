Request account deletion

Uninstalled ParkLagbee? Submit a deletion request here. We will verify that the request belongs to you before processing it.

What happens to your data

Deleted from your account

Your profile and contact details, NID or vehicle verification documents, parking listings and uploaded spot photos, reviews, and account-linked booking history are removed when the request is verified and completed.

May be retained where required

Records needed for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution obligations may be retained for the applicable period. Where possible, retained records are detached from your profile or de-identified.

Processing

This form creates a request for the ParkLagbee support team; it does not delete the account immediately. We may contact you using the details provided to verify ownership and confirm completion.

Send a deletion request

Provide the email address or phone number associated with your ParkLagbee account.

Your contact details and reason are sent directly to ParkLagbee's deletion-request queue and are used to process this request.
