These terms apply when you use ParkLagbee to find, list, request, or manage parking. By using the app, you agree to follow these rules and any booking-specific conditions shown in the app.

1. The service and accounts

ParkLagbee connects Vehicle Owners looking for parking with Spot Owners offering parking space. Unless a feature says otherwise, users arrange access and any payment directly with each other. ParkLagbee is not the owner or operator of listed spaces and cannot guarantee a user's identity, listing condition, availability, or conduct.

Keep your account credentials private and provide accurate account information. You are responsible for activity performed through your account. Do not impersonate others, evade a restriction, or use the service for unlawful activity.

2. Spot Owner responsibilities

  • List only a space you own or are authorized to offer, and keep its location, photos, price, capacity, vehicle limits, rules, and availability accurate.
  • Maintain a safe, lawful, and accessible parking area. Disclose relevant access instructions or hazards before confirming a booking.
  • Review booking requests promptly, honor confirmed bookings, and do not accept overlapping reservations for the same capacity.
  • Do not upload misleading, infringing, private, or inappropriate images or information. You must have permission to publish anything you upload.

3. Vehicle Owner responsibilities

  • Provide accurate vehicle information and select a spot suitable for your vehicle and intended use.
  • Wait for the booking to be confirmed before treating the space as reserved. Follow the Spot Owner's stated access instructions and parking rules.
  • Arrive and leave within the confirmed booking period. Do not overstay, block access, damage property, or leave prohibited or hazardous items.
  • Secure your vehicle and personal belongings. Inspect the space and contact the Spot Owner through the available booking contact details if a safety or access issue arises.

4. Booking rules

A booking request is pending until it is approved or otherwise marked confirmed in the app. A confirmed booking applies to the selected spot, vehicle, date, and time period. Both parties should communicate any access requirements before arrival and must not misrepresent a booking's status.

5. Cancellations and changes

Pending or confirmed bookings may be cancelled in the app before the booking start time. Past or ongoing bookings cannot be cancelled through the app. A cancellation releases the reserved slot. Contact the other party about schedule changes; any payment adjustment or refund must be agreed between the parties unless the app explicitly provides another process.

6. Safety, content, and reports

Use good judgment when arranging access or meeting another user. Do not share passwords or verification codes. Do not post abusive, discriminatory, fraudulent, threatening, or illegal content. Use Report Content for suspicious listings, inappropriate photos, abusive reviews, or misleading information; use Block User to hide that user's spots and reviews from your account.

ParkLagbee may review reports, remove content, restrict accounts, or suspend access when needed to protect users or enforce these terms.

7. Service availability and disputes

The app and listings may change or become temporarily unavailable. ParkLagbee is not responsible for disputes between users, the condition of a space, or losses resulting from a user's actions. Contact the other party first where appropriate, and use in-app Help & Report Issue for assistance.

8. Account deletion and changes to these terms

You may permanently delete your account from Profile settings or submit an external request through the account deletion page. We may update these terms as the service changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms.
